Each draw is one zone sample of *n* eligible aircraft where each aircraft is bad with probability *f*. A cell shows how often the frozen rule classes that sample **DEGRADED** (at least 10% bad, at least 3 bad aircraft of at least 2 types, and a Wilson lower bound of at least 0.02). ## S5

Target: at least 90% detection at 15% bad with n = 30, and at most 2% false DEGRADED at 2% bad.
Measured: **86.0%** detection and **2.8%** false. **Miss.**

The rule is frozen (docs/METHOD.md), so a miss is reported here and on the page rather than tuned away. At n = 30 a DEGRADED sample needs 3 bad aircraft; at 15% bad the average is 4.5, so some samples fall short. At 20% bad, n = 30 detects 95.7% of samples.

## False onsets

An onset needs two DEGRADED samples in a row. If a quiet zone's samples are independent, the chance of a false onset in any one slot is about the false-sample rate squared, and a zone has 144 slots a day. At 2% bad that is **0.11** false onsets per zone per day at n = 30 and 0.001 at n = 60, or about **1.4** a day across 12 sentinels if every one sat at 2% with 30 aircraft. Real samples ten minutes apart share many aircraft, so they aren't independent, and false onsets are not filtered out: every one is logged and listed. The controls measure the real rate (S3).
